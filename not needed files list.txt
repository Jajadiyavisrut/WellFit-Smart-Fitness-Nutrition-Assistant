NOTE regarding .env and init_db.py: 
You DO need the values inside `.env` (like your DATABASE_URL) to make the deployed app work, but you shouldn't upload the actual `.env` file to your hosting provider directly. Instead, you typically paste those keys into your hosting provider's "Environment Variables" dashboard.
`init_db.py` is only strictly needed to create the tables in Supabase for the first time, but not needed to actually serve the application online.
